py123detection.core.visibility¶

Which ground-truth boxes a rig’s cameras can actually see.

A camera-only detector is scored on every annotated object, including the ones behind it. On a 360-degree rig like nuScenes’ that is harmless. On Waymo’s five forward-facing cameras, which between them cover about 230 degrees, roughly a third of the annotations are objects no camera observed. Counting those makes recall look worse than the model is, for reasons that have nothing to do with the model.

RigVisibility reads the rig geometry once and then answers, per box, whether any camera could have seen it. Two tests, in increasing cost:

  • center compares the box centre’s bearing against each camera’s horizontal field of view. It ignores elevation and image bounds, and is a good default.

  • corners projects all eight corners and asks whether any lands inside an image. Stricter, and the right choice when the vertical field of view is narrow.

A rig covering the full circle short-circuits: every box is visible, and the filter costs nothing.
