py123detection.annotations¶

Per-frame 3D annotations in mmdetection3d’s nuScenes layout.

123D stores boxes in the global frame, mmdet3d wants them in the keyframe’s lidar frame: gt_boxes (N, 7) as [x, y, z, l, w, h, yaw] around the box center (mmdet3d moves the origin to the bottom face at load time) and gt_velocity (N, 2).

py123detection.annotations.BOX_LAYOUTS = ('streampetr', 'mmdet3d_0.17')¶

“streampetr”: [x, y, z, l, w, h, yaw], written and read by StreamPETR and mmdetection3d >= 1.0. “mmdet3d_0.17”: [x, y, z, w, l, h, -yaw - pi/2], written by mmdetection3d 0.17’s nuScenes converter and fed verbatim into LiDARInstance3DBoxes by its dataset (PETR v1 pins 0.17).

py123detection.annotations.build_frame_annotations(records, global_to_lidar, yaw_convention='mmdet3d', planar_velocity=True, box_layout='streampetr')[source]¶

Move global-frame records into the lidar frame and build the mmdet3d arrays.

Parameters:

  • yaw_convention (str) – See py123detection.geometry.YAW_CONVENTIONS.

  • planar_velocity (bool) – Zero the vertical velocity before rotating, as mmdetection3d does. Otherwise a tilted lidar leaks it into (vx, vy).

  • box_layout (str) – One of BOX_LAYOUTS.
